# Executive Summary: Regional Store Diagnosis & Pune Premise Test
**Dataset:** DMart Regional Store Daily (Synthetic Practice Dataset)  
**Period Covered:** 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-29 (90 days)  
**Author:** Regional Operations Analytics Team  

### Headline Finding
Kavita Rao's working premise—that Pune's regional revenue lag is caused by stockouts—is unsupported by operational data. Pune's deficit is an acute footfall volume problem.

### Magnitude
Pune's daily revenue (INR 636,358.55) is 26.14% below the all-store regional mean (INR 861,535.24). Daily footfall (1,517.71) is 25.00% below the regional average (2,023.59). Crucially, Pune's revenue per footfall (INR 419.29) is only 1.52% below the network mean (INR 425.75) and exceeds Hyderabad (INR 404.27) and Ahmedabad (INR 407.08).

### Drivers vs Non-Drivers
- **Not Driving Deficit:** Stockouts. Pune averages 2.32 stockout hours/day (4th best out of 5 stores; network average 2.74 hrs, Hyderabad worst at 3.85 hrs).
- **Driving Deficit:** Store footfall volume (r = 0.735 in Pune; r = 0.918 network-wide). 70 of Pune's 90 days fell in the Poor band (<75% target achievement).

### Supporting Facts
- Regional daily revenue correlation with footfall is 0.918; with stockout hours it is -0.152.
- Pune converts in-store traffic effectively (INR 419.29/customer).

### Open Hypotheses
- Catchment accessibility or parking bottlenecks restrict physical store visits (requires local competitor and transit foot-traffic logs).
- Local promotional campaign reach is insufficient (requires localized marketing ROI and geo-targeted impression data).

### Recommendation
- **Owner:** Regional Marketing Head
- **Action:** Halt capital deployment for Pune stock-availability programmes; redirect capital into targeted local catchment marketing drives and weekend footfall traffic activations.
- **Timeline:** 14 days.
